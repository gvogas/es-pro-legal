ES Pro — Accessibility

Scheduling app for managing appointments, clients, and employees · by Plombier Eau Secours
Applies to ES Pro for iOS, version 1.37 · Last updated July 31, 2026

ES Pro is a working tool: plumbers use it on a phone, outdoors, in a van, with gloves on. Making it readable and operable for everyone is part of making it usable at all. This page describes what ES Pro supports today, what it does not, and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

Everything below is available to every user of the app. There is no separate “accessible mode,” and no accessibility feature is behind a paid tier.

Apple accessibility features in ES Pro

Support for the accessibility features described by Apple’s Accessibility Nutrition Labels.
FeatureSupportDetails
VoiceOver Supported Buttons, rows, form fields, and pickers carry spoken labels in both English and French. An appointment is announced as one summary — client, time, crew, status — instead of a stream of fragments. A calendar day announces its full date plus how many appointments it holds, because the coloured dots on the day cannot be spoken. Logos, decorative graphics, and the weekday header row are hidden from VoiceOver so they do not interrupt reading.
Voice Control Supported Controls are standard iOS controls with the same names VoiceOver reads, so “tap Save”, “show names”, and the numbered overlay work throughout the app. Buttons and list rows use full-size tap targets.
Larger / adjustable text Supported ES Pro follows the iOS Larger Text setting, and adds its own text size setting (Small, Medium, Large, XL) so you can go bigger still without changing every other app on the phone. The two combine, up to about 2.2× the default size. Screens are laid out to reflow — not clip — at double text size.
Dark interface Supported The app follows the iOS appearance setting by default, and can be pinned to light or dark from inside the app. Both themes are fully designed — dark is not an inverted copy.
Differentiate without colour alone Supported Colour is always a second signal, never the only one. Job status is a labelled badge (“Scheduled”, “In progress”, “Complete”, “Cancelled”), not just a colour. Crew colours sit beside the crew members’ names. The offline state is a banner with text. Where a coloured dot is the only visual marker — the crew dots on the month calendar — its meaning is spoken as a count.
Sufficient contrast Supported The light and dark palettes were built to keep body text, labels, and essential controls above the WCAG 2.1 AA contrast thresholds (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI shapes). Muted secondary text is used for supporting detail only, never to carry information that is not repeated elsewhere.
Reduced motion Supported Every animation in the app checks the iOS Reduce Motion setting and turns itself off: screen and sheet transitions, calendar month paging, loading shimmers, chart animations, photo-viewer gestures, form field transitions, and the guided tour all become instant.
Captions Not applicable ES Pro contains no video or prerecorded audio. Job photos are still images.
Audio descriptions Not applicable ES Pro contains no video content.

Accessibility settings inside ES Pro

Open Settings from the menu in the app:

  • Text size — Small, Medium, Large, or XL, with a live preview. This applies across the whole app and stacks on top of the iOS Larger Text setting.
  • Appearance — follow the system, or force light or dark.
  • Language — English or French, independent of the device language. Spoken VoiceOver labels, notifications, and Siri responses follow the language you choose.
  • Notifications — a shortcut that takes you straight to the iOS notification settings for ES Pro if reminders are not arriving.
  • Replay app tour — restarts the guided walkthrough of each screen.

Turning on iOS accessibility features

These are device settings; they apply to ES Pro as soon as you enable them.

  • VoiceOver — Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver
  • Voice Control — Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control
  • Larger Text — Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text
  • Bold Text — Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Bold Text
  • Increase Contrast — Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Increase Contrast
  • Reduce Motion — Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion
  • Dark appearance — Settings → Display & Brightness → Dark

Known limitations

We would rather tell you where the app falls short than let you discover it on a job site.

  • The live crew map. The map itself is a standard Google map, and its pins are not individually reachable with VoiceOver. The same information is available as text: open the staff roster sheet from the map, which lists each person with their name, how recent their location is, and how far away they are, nearest first.
  • Charts. The weekly bar chart on the dashboard is announced as a text summary rather than bar by bar. The numbers behind it are also shown as text beside the chart.
  • Status badges stop growing. Short status badges stop scaling at roughly 1.3× so they stay on a single line and remain legible. Every other piece of text keeps scaling.
  • Job photos have no descriptions. Photos attached to a job are announced as images without a description of their contents, because they are taken on site by crew members and carry no caption.
  • Verification is ongoing. We test with VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and enlarged text on the main flows — signing in, reading the schedule, opening and completing a job — but we have not audited every screen end to end. If a screen is unclear or unreachable, please tell us; we treat it as a bug, not a request.

Assistive technology we test with

  • VoiceOver on iPhone (iOS 18)
  • Larger Text at maximum, plus the in-app XL setting
  • Reduce Motion
  • Dark and light appearance

Accessibility feedback

If any part of ES Pro is difficult or impossible to use, email george@vogas.net with Accessibility in the subject line. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

It helps to include: what you were trying to do, which screen you were on, which assistive technology or setting you use (for example VoiceOver or Larger Text), and your device model and iOS version.

We will confirm what we can reproduce, tell you whether a fix is planned, and follow up when it ships.
